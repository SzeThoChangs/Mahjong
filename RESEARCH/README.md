# RESEARCH/

The material behind `RESEARCH.md`, which is the register. Read the register first; open this
directory when the register is not enough.

## Where the existing material actually lives

This project had three weeks of investigation behind it before the P-Starter structure was laid
down on 2026-09-12, and that material was left where it was rather than moved here. Moving it
would have broken references in the code, the commit history and every other document, for no gain.
`RESEARCH/manifest.json` lists all of it with its real path, and the project interface reads that
manifest, so nothing is hidden by the decision.

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `../FINDINGS.md` | This project's own measurements. Every experiment it has run, what it showed, and what was wrong about the argument that preceded it. The largest single piece of research material here. |
| `../knowledge/` | The combined playbook: 102 rules across seven decision phases, machine-readable beside it, with per-source coverage notes in `knowledge/sources/`. |
| `../136 ready to use MJ strategy and tactics/` | Copyrighted source material. Git-ignored and it stays that way. Registered by reference only; never copy its contents into a tracked file. |
| `../Data Analytic Evluation of Singapore Mahjong/` | Copyrighted source material. Same rule. |

## What goes in here from now on

New investigation: a written-up study, a literature search, a comparison of options, a measurement
report that is too long to belong in `FINDINGS.md`. One document per investigation, named by date
and subject. Register it in `RESEARCH.md` and add it to the manifest.

Research material is findings, not requirements. Nothing in here is a decision or a rule until it
is accepted into the definition files.
